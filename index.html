The invention

Invention Report 2541: “The Design of an Internet-Based Immersive 3-D Digital Orthopedics Learning Environment Using Augmented Reality.”

The concept connects immersive technology with instructional design for distributed orthopedic education. Its research context includes realistic decision-making, feedback, cognitive fit, haptics, simulation-based learning, and access in resource-constrained settings.
